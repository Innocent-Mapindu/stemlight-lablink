Ohm's Law and I–V Characteristics

Aim: measure how the current through a component changes with the potential difference across it, for a fixed resistor and a filament lamp, and find the resistance.

Circuit board tap a terminal, then another, to connect a wire

Tap the switch to open or close it. Red terminals are positive (+), dark terminals are negative (−).

Circuit diagram and procedure

  1. Connect the battery, switch, rheostat, ammeter and component in series. Connect + on the ammeter towards + on the battery.
  2. Connect the voltmeter in parallel across the component, + to the side nearer the battery's +.
  3. Close the switch. Adjust the rheostat (and the number of cells) to get at least 6 different readings.
  4. Record V and I each time, then open the switch so the component does not heat up between readings.
  5. Plot I against V. For the resistor, R = 1 ÷ gradient. Repeat with the filament lamp and compare the shapes.
