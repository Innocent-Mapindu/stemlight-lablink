The Simple Pendulum

Aim: determine the acceleration due to gravity, g, by measuring the period T for several lengths l and plotting T² against l.

Apparatus clamp · thread · bob · rule · stopwatch

Procedure

  1. Set the length l (from the point of suspension to the centre of the bob).
  2. Displace the bob through a small angle (under 10°) and release it.
  3. Start the stopwatch as the bob passes the fiducial marker, count 20 oscillations, then stop it.
  4. Record the reading. Repeat for at least 5 different lengths.
  5. Plot T² against l, find the gradient, and calculate g = 4π² ÷ gradient.
